    FAQ

    Answers to the questions
    that come up most.

    Who is medNAMA for?

    Candidates preparing for FCPS Part 1, the basic sciences paper. It’s also useful for MBBS revision, and Dr MedNama can pitch answers for FCPS-II.

    Where do the answers come from?

    From a library of standard medical textbooks. Every textbook fact is cited with the book and page; anything beyond the books is labelled as AI knowledge, so you always know which is which.

    Can it be wrong?

    Yes. Any AI can make mistakes, which is why medNAMA shows its sources and puts a Report button on every answer and question. Answer keys that the textbooks contradict are flagged and reviewed.

    Are the past papers official?

    No. They are questions recalled by candidates after each sitting, collected and matched across years. They show what tends to be asked, not CPSP’s official papers.

    How will I get medNAMA?

    As an app you install on your own computer, with the textbook packs you choose, plus a web version for your phone and other devices. Details come with the launch.

    What will it cost?

    Pricing is being finalised. The plan is per textbook pack, so you pay for the books you actually study from.

    Who can see my progress?

    Only you. Other students see a shortened version of your name on a weekly mock leaderboard, and “how others did” only appears once enough students take part.

What medNAMA does today.
Seven jobs, one place.

AskDr MedNama

Ask anything and get an answer written from the textbooks, with every claim tied to a book and printed page. A reference the model cannot back up is removed before you see it.

It reads what kind of question you asked (a definition, a mechanism, management) and answers that, at the depth of your exam. Or switch to Tutor me and be questioned step by step.

ReviseSheets and notes

One-page revision sheets from the books and chapters you choose: core concepts, numbers and values, classic presentations, look-alikes and traps.

Every bullet carries its reference, and AI-made mnemonics are labelled as such. No set number of bullets: a narrow topic gets a short sheet.

PractiseBank, papers, mocks

Build a paper the way it is sat: Paper 1 by subject and topic, Paper 2 by specialty, from the bank and past papers together, with an easier-to-harder dial based on real results.

Go through the past-paper archive by year, sit the weekly mock, drill your missed questions, or challenge a friend to a duel.

RememberDaily Dose and re-tests

A ten-minute Daily Dose every day: due re-tests, your weakest subject, an image and a pearl. Re-tests are spaced by FSRS so you review just before you would forget.

Mistakes are sorted into look-alike mix-ups, confident errors and real gaps, because each needs a different fix.

CheckAnswer keys

Paste or photograph a recall question and the referee tells you what the textbooks say, quoting the page. It settles the WhatsApp-group argument with evidence.

When two standard books disagree, you see both side by side. Checked questions that medNAMA did not hold are added to the bank for everyone.

PlanToday

One clear next step each day, from your paper’s official blueprint: weak topics first, then the topics the paper asks most, with a weekly target that finishes the paper before your exam date.

A readiness forecast against the pass line, a streak, an exam countdown, and a mock built to the official counts when you’re ready.

RehearseViva and TOACS

Examiner-led viva stations: one question at a time, guided with hints or timed like the real thing, then a debrief that marks each criterion and quotes your words.

Paediatrics is the pilot. It is an educational simulation, never an official result, and stations say “Practice only” until their marking is validated.

How an answer is made.
The books first, every time.

The same pipeline sits behind chat, revision sheets and question writing, so all three agree with each other and with the page.

UnderstandReads what the question asks and which exam you are preparing for.
SearchMeaning-based and keyword search together, across every book in scope.
RerankA second model reads each candidate passage against the question.
DiversifyMakes room for every book that covers the point, not just the longest one.
WriteAnswers only from those passages; anything else is labelled as an AI supplement.
VerifyChecks every citation against the pages actually retrieved; unbacked ones are removed.

Behind the scenes.
Measured, not guessed.

Changes to how medNAMA finds and writes answers are tested before students see them. Two tools do most of that work.

A retrieval benchmark

67 real exam questions, each with the passages a correct answer must come from, run through every candidate setup. The aim: put the right page first.

first-place hit rate on the benchmark
specialist medical model0.67
current setup0.77
best candidate0.89

A specialist medical reranker scored lower than expected; the best general model put the right page first in 89% of questions.

A question-writing studio

Practice for an exam is planned from its official Table of Specifications: every topic and sub-topic, with the number of questions the paper gives it, and a bank target several times larger.

Every generated question is checked against its source page, compared with the whole bank for duplicates, and held for review before any student sees it. Rejected questions are kept with the reason, so the numbers stay honest.
